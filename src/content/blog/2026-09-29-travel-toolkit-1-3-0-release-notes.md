Travel Toolkit 1.3.0 adds a place to explore articles and release updates, while
making trip costs easier to understand and several everyday workflows smoother.

## A new place for articles

The Travel Toolkit blog is now available for articles and changelog updates.
Browse posts by tag to find topics that interest you.

## Clearer trip planning

Trip summaries now show the total for the whole trip alongside each group's
share, making it easier to see both the overall cost and how it is divided.

When swapping save slots, you can now clear a selected slot. Starting a new trip
also gives clearer information about save status before you continue.

The Trip Cost experience now has clearer page structure, keyboard interactions,
and contextual themed tooltips to make controls easier to navigate and
understand. Timezone search and selection have also been improved.

## A mobile banner fix

The consent banner now stays above the mobile tabs, so it remains visible on
smaller screens.
