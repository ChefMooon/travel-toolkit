Travel Toolkit has a new look! Version 1.5.0 brings the feeling of a travel
postcard across the site, with warm paper textures, stamp-inspired icons, and
refreshed navigation. It's a new backdrop for the tools you use to plan your
trips.

## Your journey starts on the homepage

The homepage sets the scene with a world map and an animated flight path.
Postcard-style tool cards and colorful stamp icons make it easy to spot where
you want to go next, whether you're working out trip costs or checking a
destination forecast.

## A fresh look throughout the toolkit

The new look extends beyond the homepage. Open the tool menu to see the tools
in a list, with your current tool marked so you can keep your bearings. The
Trip Cost toolbar and mobile navigation have been refreshed too, bringing the
postcard theme along as you move between tools.

Light and dark themes have improved text contrast for easier reading, while
the app icon and browser appearance complete the new look. We hope you enjoy
exploring it!
