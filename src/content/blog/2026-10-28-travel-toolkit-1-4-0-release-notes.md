Travel Toolkit 1.4.0 adds a clearer way to keep track of trip timing, along with a dedicated converter for the measurements that come up while traveling.

## Keep your trip dates in view

Add start and end dates to a trip with the shared date picker, or use its quick actions to choose today or clear a date. The Trip Cost page now shows a countdown strip with the time until departure or the days remaining once your trip is underway.

The new Travel Countdown tool also works on its own: enter your dates to see how soon the trip starts, how long is left, or how long ago it ended.

## Convert common travel measurements

The new Unit Converter handles temperature, distance, weight, and volume. Choose the units you need—for example, convert a distance between kilometers and miles or a temperature between Celsius and Fahrenheit—and swap the input and result units when you want to compare in the other direction.

## Set preferences for your travels

Global travel preferences now include a preferred trip region, and Travel Toolkit detects your browser language the first time you visit. French (Canada) is available as a translation review preview; some text may be incomplete or need correction.

The release also improves unit-conversion feedback and refines the homepage, navigation, and mobile readability.
