Travel Toolkit 1.5.1 adds French and Spanish translations and gives saved trips
a new look. It also fixes a couple of language and empty-trip bugs.

## French and Spanish

The interface is now available in French (France), Spanish (Spain), and
Spanish (Mexico). If your browser is set to French or Spanish, the app picks
the matching variant automatically.

Language loading is more forgiving too. If a translation takes too long to
load, the app falls back to English. If a locale fails to load, your saved
language preference is kept instead of being reset.

## Saved trips as boarding passes

Saved-trip slots are now styled as boarding-pass tickets. Empty trips are also
labeled as empty. Before, they were reported as missing an exchange rate.

## Smaller changes

Trip sections now have custom travel-stamp illustrations, and the homepage has
a localized subtitle under its title. Tool layouts, navigation, and hover
states have been tidied up to fit the postcard look from 1.5.0, and action
feedback, motion, trip totals, and exports are more consistent across tools.
