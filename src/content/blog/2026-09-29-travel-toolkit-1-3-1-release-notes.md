Travel Toolkit 1.3.1 makes a few everyday interactions smoother, from entering
trip costs to understanding on-screen guidance.

## Add costs faster

You can now duplicate a cost directly from its row in the Cost Ledger. This is
useful when several expenses share similar details: start with an existing
entry instead of entering everything again.

## Clearer interactions

Confirmation dialogs now cover the full screen, making it easier to focus on
the decision you need to make. Tooltips also dismiss when you move the pointer
away from the control that opened them.

Blog post title links have also received a visual refinement for a clearer
appearance.
