Travel Toolkit 1.3.3 makes trip editing easier to recover and brings a round of
usability refinements to the tools and the rest of the site.

## More confidence while editing a trip

You can now undo and redo trip edits with keyboard shortcuts, and you'll get a
warning if you try to leave with changes that haven't been saved. Trip-cost
fields and controls have also been refined to work more comfortably across
screen sizes.

## Smoother travel tools

The currency and timezone tools now use more consistent controls. Currency
rates are more accurate, timezone tracking is clearer, and destination
forecast search has a more polished layout. Forecast attribution tooltips also
stay within the visible screen.

## A more consistent experience

Your theme preference now carries across the site without being tied to trip
data, and trip summaries have improved contrast. Homepage and blog interactions,
navigation feedback, and motion have also been refined.
