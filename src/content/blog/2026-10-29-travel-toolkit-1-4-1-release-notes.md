Travel Toolkit 1.4.1 refines the homepage layout so the tool launcher feels more balanced.

## A more balanced tool launcher

The homepage tool cards now sit within a narrower, centered grid instead of spreading across as much horizontal space on wide screens. This keeps the launcher visually grouped as you browse the available travel tools.
